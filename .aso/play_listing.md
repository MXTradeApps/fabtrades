# Google Play listing (paste into Play Console)

Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing. Add each language under
"Manage translations → Add your own translations". Play allows "free" in the short description;
it does not allow it in the app name.

| --- | --- | --- |

| Spanish (es-419) | FAB Trades: Flesh and Blood | Intercambios gratis, escáner de cartas y precios diarios para Flesh and Blood |
| Italian (it-IT) | FAB Trades: Flesh and Blood | Scambi gratis, scanner di carte e prezzi giornalieri per Flesh and Blood |

Full description: use the `description` for the same language in `.aso/listing.json`
(es-MX for es-419). Play's limit is 4000 characters; every description there is under 2,800.
